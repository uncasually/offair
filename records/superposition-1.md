ALTERED is born from a shared desire to craft a personal, introspective, deep listening experience. Our goal is to immerse the listener in a multi-dimensional soundscape, inviting them to lose and find themselves in layers of continuously evolving, mosaic textures. Although each track stands on its own, this album represents a single arc, designed to be ideally experienced from beginning to end. 

The Grammy-nominated duo of Justin Boreta and Matthew Davis recognized their "shared desire to craft a personal, introspective, deep listening experience" in this new project*Altered*, out August 23rd. We envision listeners engaging with this work lying down, eyes closed, with headphones on, embracing a space of focused auditory exploration.
